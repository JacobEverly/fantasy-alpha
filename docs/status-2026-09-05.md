## Result

The adapter clearly learned the corpus (development NLL 1.257→0.815) and had a
promising +81.15 mean masked-draft reward change, but only won 2/6 paired drafts.
Prediction results were effectively flat: BreakoutBench regressed 0.0009 Brier;
CalibBench improved 0.0013 Brier while log loss worsened. The paired aggregate
interval includes zero. Structured output stayed perfect and the canary passed.

T1.1 drove development NLL from 1.727 to 0.111 but over-specialized: it matched
the control reward in 21/24 drafts, achieved 52.1% structured coverage, made
zero tool calls across 246 opportunities, and introduced three illegal picks.
It went 12/24 against base and 13/24 against T1, missing the precommitted 60%
win requirement against both. Six of eight decision gates failed.

T1.2 retained 100% parseable response schemas and the revised canary improved
development target matching from 43.8% to 65.6%. However, both canaries chose a
pick on all three unseen states that required a depth-chart lookup. The revised
canary therefore had 0% valid tool calls and three wrong-type actions despite a
development NLL improvement from 1.157 to 0.794.

Frozen verdict: **stop additional SFT; do not begin RL**. The full T1.2 adapter
and paid four-way evaluation were intentionally not run because the
precommitted canary gate failed twice.
