## What we learned (the scoreboard)

| Finding | Number | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| Market is strong | naive drafting −141 pts/season vs ADP | The bar is real |
| No historical selection alpha | all lifts bracket 1.0x (GBDT, Qwen, ±harness, ±enrichment) | The market prices everything reconstructable about the past — killed the weak thesis **before** spending on it |
| Harness fixes calibration | Brier 0.49 → 0.24; enrichment *hurts* unanchored models | Grounded updating is trainable and valuable |
| Execution layer works | rescues 60% of bad-projection damage; halves variance | Decision quality has headroom independent of prediction |

| Timing economics implemented | round-7 action on an 8th-round breakout = 11x the value of a round-1 reach | Incentive-correct scoring per Jacob's rule |

**Strategic consequence:** training targets shift to what evidence supports — sequential decision quality (DraftGym RL), calibrated evidence-anchored analysis + sourcing behavior (SFT), and the in-season loop. Selection alpha gets proven prospectively: **2026 ledger, predictions frozen by Sep 4.**
