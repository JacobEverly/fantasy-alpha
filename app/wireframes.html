03

Chat — the core surface

Job: verdict first, receipts one tap below. The visual signature of the product is that every answer carries its evidence — tool outputs, dated sources, a freshness stamp, and an honest confidence label.

Why receipts win: stale/hallucinated data is the #1 complaint about AI fantasy tools (wrong teams, backups listed as starters). Raw ChatGPT can't show a projection distribution or your league's replacement level — this layout makes the difference impossible to miss.

Public ledger

Job: the moat. A trading track record, not a blog — dated probabilistic calls with the ADP "entry price," scored against reality, misses left on the board, benchmarked against frontier models.

Marketing honesty rule (GAMEPLAN §4): historical backtests are contamination-suspect by construction. The timestamped 2026 ledger is the only proof we lead with — so this page has to look like an audited record, not content marketing.
