# Packet v2.1 — feature coverage (2026-08-11)

Generated by `evals/packet_features.py` after two changes; tables are
`data/processed/packet_features/features_<S>.csv`, rebuilt for 2015–2024.
As-of gate unchanged: every value from data strictly before Sept 1 of S.

**What changed in v2.1**

1. **Role-quality channel (1b)** — new S−1 aggregates from the 145-col
   nflverse weekly files (`receiving_air_yards`, `wopr`, `target_share`,
   `air_yards_share` — all real from 2010+, zero-filled before 2006):
   - `adot_s1/_s2/_yoy` — receiving air yards per target (aDOT-ish); only
     emitted when the season had >0 targets and non-NA air-yards rows
   - `wopr_s1/_s2/_yoy` — mean weekly WOPR (nflverse precomputed)
   - `target_share_s2/_yoy`, `air_yards_share_s2/_yoy` — YoY deltas for the
     existing share features; a delta is emitted only when both sides exist
   - `tgt_games_s1`, `route_part_proxy_s1` — games with ≥1 target, and that
     count over the team's REG games (no route/snap columns exist in this
     schema, so "in the target rotation, availability included" is the proxy)
   - `role_status`: ok / no_prior_season / not_derivable. Missing ≠ zero
     throughout (zero-target aDOT is empty, never 0.0).
   - **No red-zone / end-zone proxy**: the weekly schema carries no RZ/EZ
     opportunity columns (only TD outcomes), so none is emitted.
2. **News lexicon re-cut** — `NEWS_LEXICON_VERSION = packet-news-lexicon-v2`.
   v1's bare `\bstarter\b|\bstarting\b` fired on preseason-logistics prose
   ("starters will play a quarter Thursday"; ~82% false-fire measured in
   `evals/source_alpha.py`). v2 ports source_alpha's promotion-specific
   `CAMP_PROMOTION_RE` (`source-alpha-lexicon-v1`) verbatim. Counts across
   lexicon versions are not comparable; do not mix pre/post tables.

## camp_promotion_flags: lexicon v1 → v2

Sum of `camp_promotion_flags` over all rows per season (window: 90 days
before Sept 1). Net effect = bare-starter false fires removed, minus new
true-promotion terms added (promoted/promotion, TE1/QB1, "lead role",
"no. 1 receiver", "atop the depth chart").

| 2016 | 95 | 49 | 48.4% |

| 2023 | 356 | 277 | 22.2% |
| 2024 | 412 | 307 | 25.5% |
| **total** | **2916** | **2144** | **26.5%** |

The net drop (26.5%) is smaller than source_alpha's ~82% false-fire rate on
its candidate blurbs because (a) packet news text is short Sleeper
title+description strings, not full blurb prose, so bare "starter" fired
less often here to begin with, and (b) v2 also *adds* promotion terms that
v1 missed. 2015 has no news coverage (`pre_coverage`) in both versions.
