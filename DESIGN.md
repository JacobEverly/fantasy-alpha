## Project Identity

**Name**: Fantasy Alpha
**Tagline**: The analyst that shows its work.
**Audience**: Sharp-curious fantasy football players who want an edge they can verify.
**Goal**: League setup (7 fields) → first question answered with receipts.

## Personality Layer

### Display Font
**Space Grotesk** (700, tracking -0.02em) — geometric and slightly technical without being cold; reads "terminal" next to Inter body without resorting to monospace headlines.
- Where to use: hero heading, section headings, the wordmark, big stat callouts.
- Where NOT to use: body, chat messages, table content, nav, buttons (all Inter).

**Data figures**: IBM Plex Mono, tabular-lined, for **numeric data values only** — ADP, VORP, percentages, Brier scores, timestamps, ledger entries. This is the Linear monospace-accent pattern with a functional justification (columns of numbers must align), not monospace-as-decoration. Never for headings, body prose, or UI labels.

### Accent Palette
Dark-first site on the base theme's dark scale (`--bg: #0D0D0D`, `--fg: #FAFAFA`, borders `#2A2A2A`, surfaces `#1A1A1A`).

```css
--accent-primary: #10B981;   /* market green — wordmark moment, active states in data viz, the "live" dot on the ledger. Never chrome, never buttons. */
--gain:           #34D399;   /* semantic only: positive alpha, hit calls, VORP-above bars */
--loss:           #F87171;   /* semantic only: negative alpha, missed calls, busts */
--warn:           #FBBF24;   /* semantic only: injury/volatility flags */
```

Rules: primary buttons stay near-white-on-dark (inverted near-black per base). Gain/loss colors appear exclusively inside data (bars, deltas, badges) — a page with no data shows almost no color. No neon values; these are the desaturated ends of their families.

### Motion Style
**Subtle in-app, moderate on landing.**
- Page load (landing): fade-up entrances 400–600ms, expo-out, 60–80ms stagger; hero canvas (ambient floating dated predictions/ADP deltas) drifts at ≤0.2px/frame.
- Landing benchmark section: scroll-triggered **animated comparison bars** with count-up numbers (library pattern) — the one theatrical moment.
- In-app (chat, draft board, ledger): NO entrance animation on data. Number changes count-up ≤300ms; hover = background shift 100–150ms; evidence cards expand 200ms ease-out. Draft mode is a 90-second-decision surface — nothing may move unless the user caused it.
- Smooth scroll (lenis) on landing only; native scroll in-app.

### Unique Components
1. **Evidence card ("receipt")** — collapsible card under every chat answer: projection distribution strip, league-native VORP (mono), ADP-survival line ("available at your pick 31 in 68% of drafts"), dated source citations ("Beat report · The Athletic · Aug 6"), verbal confidence tag. Surface `#1A1A1A`, no border/shadow per base; hierarchy via opacity.
2. **Ledger row** — trading-record row: mono timestamp, call text, ADP-at-call, current status badge (`--gain` hit / `--loss` miss / neutral open). Tabular, dense, zebra via surface alternation.
3. **Survival bar** — thin horizontal probability bar for "makes it back to you," mono percentage label.
4. **Turn card** — draft board grouping for adjacent picks ("Picks 18 + 19") with tiered candidates and VORP bars.
5. **Confidence tag** — verbal scale only (high / moderate / speculative), small caps, never a percentage.
6. **Meter pill** — "7 free questions left this week," quiet, honest, top-right of chat.
7. **Freshness stamp** — "data as of 2:14 PM" on every stat surface; mono, `--fg-faint`.

## Surface Plan (v1 — reframed 2026-08-08: the app IS the product; no marketing landing)

Jacob's directive: a clean interface someone can use immediately — input league data easily on a phone, ask questions, get nerdy answers with the math shown. No product-explainer sections.

1. **Setup** — mobile-first, under 60 seconds: teams, scoring chips, roster steppers, draft slot; live snake-summary chip ("12-team · Half PPR · picks 7, 18, 31…"). Opens straight into the app.
2. **Chat** — question box + quick chips; every answer is a verdict sentence followed by its receipt card (real engine numbers: projection, league-native VORP, survival odds, freshness stamp).
3. **Draft board ("on the clock")** — recommendation card whose "why" is the turn math: value now vs. expected best-available at your next pick, survival percentages on the alternatives, tier drop-off cost. One-tap mark-taken with undo; roster rail.

**Deferred** (data accrues meanwhile; ship later): marketing landing, public ledger page, benchmark leaderboard section, pricing page.

## Image Direction
- **No photography.** Product UI and data are the imagery.
- Hero: ambient canvas of floating dated micro-predictions and ADP deltas (library ambient-canvas pattern, monochrome + rare `--accent-primary` glints).
- Section images: real product surfaces (receipts card, ledger table, draft board) rendered as clean frames — depth from contrast per base, no fake browser chrome.
- Icons: none next to headings (base rule); minimal 1.5px line icons inside UI where function demands (expand, timestamp, status).

## Copy Voice
Analyst's memo, not sports radio.
- Headlines: short declaratives. "Every call, timestamped." "Show the work." No puns, no exclamation points.
- Body: plain, quantitative, first-person-plural sparingly; numbers inline with mono styling; claims always carry their evidence or their date.
- CTAs: verb-first and literal — "Set up your league," "Read the ledger," "See the methodology." Never "Get started" or "Unleash."
- Honesty tics as brand: base rates cited next to hit rates; "we were wrong about X" appears in the ledger unhidden.

## Hard Rules (base + project)
All base-theme "Never Do" items, plus:
- Gain/loss/warn colors never appear on chrome — data only.
- Every prediction, stat, or recommendation surface carries a timestamp; every data view carries a freshness stamp.
- Confidence is verbal and calibrated; percentage-precision confidence theater is banned.
- Monospace strictly for tabular data values and timestamps.
- No sports-media urgency styling (red banners, pulsing badges, countdown pressure).
- In-app data surfaces never animate uninvited.
- Metering UI is always visible and honest; no trial-trap or hidden-renewal patterns.
